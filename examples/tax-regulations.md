# Example Tax Regulations

> These are placeholder rules for development/testing only. They are not legal or tax advice.

## Clause 1 — Revenue
Revenue from ordinary business sales is treated as taxable income.

## Clause 2 — Operating expenses
Ordinary and necessary operating expenses may be deductible.

## Clause 3 — Capital expenditure
Capital expenditure is not treated as an ordinary deductible expense under this example rule set.
